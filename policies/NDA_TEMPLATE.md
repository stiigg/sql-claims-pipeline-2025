# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (Template)

**Parties:**
- Disclosing Party: _________________________

**Purpose:** Facilitate analysis of protected health information (PHI) and associated deliverables described in the engagement proposal.

## Key Terms
- **Confidential Information:** Includes all PHI, data dictionaries, derived analytics, code, and documentation.
- **Permitted Use:** Limited to delivering analytics and remediation recommendations defined in the Statement of Work.
- **Exclusions:** Information that becomes public without breach, was already known, or independently developed without use of Confidential Information.
- **Security Controls:** Receiving Party shall maintain safeguards at least as protective as those outlined in `docs/ENV_ATTESTATION.md`.
- **Term:** Confidentiality obligations survive five (5) years after termination, or as otherwise required by applicable law.
- **Return/Destruction:** Upon request or engagement completion, Receiving Party must destroy or return Confidential Information and provide a destruction certificate (`policies/DATA_DESTRUCTION_CERT_TEMPLATE.md`).
