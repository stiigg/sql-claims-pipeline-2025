# Data Use Agreement (Template)

**Parties**
- Covered Entity: _________________________
- Business Associate / Contractor: _________________________

## Scope of Data
- **Permitted Data Elements:** Claims data, eligibility records, and enrollment metadata required for analytics deliverables.
- **Prohibited Data Elements:** Genetic data, psychotherapy notes, and any data outside agreed scope without written amendment.

## Permitted Uses & Disclosures
- Analytics, quality improvement, and compliance reporting as outlined in `docs/DELIVERABLES.md`.
- De-identified benchmarking once expressly authorized by the Covered Entity.

## Safeguards
- Administrative: Runbook and RACI defined in `docs/RUNBOOK_RACI.md`.
- Technical: Encryption, access controls, and audit logging in `docs/ENV_ATTESTATION.md`.
- Physical: Access restricted to secured facilities and managed devices.

## Breach Notification
- Contractor shall notify Covered Entity within 24 hours of discovering any security incident involving PHI.
- Incident response records shall be stored alongside the audit bundle.

## Term & Termination
- Agreement remains in effect until deliverables completed and destruction certificate delivered.
- Upon termination, Contractor must certify destruction per `policies/DATA_DESTRUCTION_CERT_TEMPLATE.md`.

_Sample language. Consult legal counsel for jurisdiction-specific requirements._
